## Risk notes

1. **Pages 30–33 are the chapter's gamble.** Four wordless pages of a man watching people work. If
   the process sequence on pp. 13–21 has not made rope genuinely interesting, this collapses. It is
   the right gamble for this series and it is also the first thing to cut if a test reader stalls.
2. **Panel average 3.0 is low for a first chapter.** That is the promise being made about pace. If
   the serialisation wants a faster opening, the fix is the 32-page cut in `chapter.md`, not
   squeezing more panels onto these pages.
3. **Page 39's hemp yard is the only plot evidence in the chapter.** If it is not staged clearly
   enough to compare against p. 14, the ending does not land. Both panels must use the **same angle
   and the same framing** — flag this to the art department as a matched pair.
