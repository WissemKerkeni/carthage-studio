## How the silence is made to work

Four devices, none of them dialogue:

1. **Duration is made measurable.** Page 32 uses the laying cart as a ruler: level with the first post, then far down the row, with the finished cable lying behind it. The tar pot stays fixed in frame as a reference marker. The reader can *see* how much rope was made while he stood there — which is the storyboard's *"the time it takes to lay ten paces of cable."*
2. **He is the only static thing.** Everything else pays out, travels, or bends. He does not move for five pages.
3. **Page 33 is two near-identical wides.** The reader compares them and finds that the only difference is the shadow, longer and gone to solid black. Nothing else has changed. That is the silence, drawn.
4. **Nobody acknowledges him** until the last page, and then only with eyes.

## Page 34 — the turn out

Her eyes lift, she sees his hands doing nothing, her eyes go back down. Her expression is identical
in panels 1 and 3 — `CHR-12` do-not-drift §3, *she is not spirited*, and §4, *she watches hands,
not faces.* The panel between them is her point of view, and it hands straight to p. 35's thesis.
