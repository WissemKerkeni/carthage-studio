**She is introduced by her hands, before her face** — `script.md`, scene 2. The reader meets a
trade before they meet a person.

> **v001 rejected.** Both hands plus the fid produced three or four overlapping hand masses,
> anatomically unreadable — the same failure as the first E1 attempt. Regenerated with **one hand
> only** and it resolved. *Extreme close-ups of two hands doing complex work are this pipeline's
> weakest case.*
