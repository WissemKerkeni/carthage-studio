## The spread

First double-page spread in the project. Built as one continuous image across both pages, split at
the trim, **page 5 taking the right half and page 6 the left** under right-to-left binding.
`manga-style.md` allows Book One exactly two spreads; this is the first, the other is ch. 4.

> **v001 rejected — `LOC-01` do-not-drift §1.** The city came back as a low, bright, spread-out
> hill-town of two and three storeys. The rule is explicit: *"Six storeys, leaning. Tyre is vertical
> and crowded. It is never drawn as a low classical town."*
>
> v002 pushes verticality and density hard — a sheer packed cliff of leaning five- and six-storey
> tenements rising straight from the water, black alley-slots cut between them, washing strung
> across the upper floors, the ships dwarfed at the waterline. **Do-not-drift §2 also satisfied:**
> the two bronze pillars stand above the rooftops.

## Narration — p. 9 only

`CANON-SOURCE`, and the scene's only words:

> Tyre smelled of dead shellfish, and everybody who lived there had stopped noticing.

**The caption is placed over the man eating his lunch**, exactly as `storyboard.md` beat 8 asks.
He is not reacting to the vat beside him, and that is the whole joke and the whole point.

## The quiet canon beat, p. 10

Two foreign officials sit at their own table among the Tyrian clerks, with their own tablets, a
guard behind them, entirely at ease — and **not one Tyrian in the panel is looking at them.**
Nobody names them, no caption explains them. `world.md`: Tyre is the richest place in the world,
and it is frightened.
