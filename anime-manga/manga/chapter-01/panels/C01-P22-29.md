## Pages 27–28 — the chapter's method in miniature

The storyboard's instruction is four words: **"Eyes move; hands do not."** The pages are built as
strict alternation — a face, then the hands under it; a second face, then those hands — and the
rhythm tightens from medium on p. 27 to extreme close-up on p. 28. The wide at the foot of p. 27
confirms it: **not one person in the lane has stopped.**

This is `world.md`'s Tyre drawn rather than stated. A priest walks into a working lane alone and
nobody can afford to look up.

## Page 29 — the gesture

No face. No words. Three panels: the sandals stopping beside a black crust of spilled tar, a soft
uncallused hand gathering the hem clear of it, and the tar itself.

`CHR-33` do-not-drift §3: *dressed down but obviously rich — the cloth is plain and the quality is
unmistakable.* And from his wardrobe note: **"He lifts the hem over the tar. That gesture is the
character."** The page is only that gesture.

**The 180-degree line is set here** and holds through p. 38.
