# Chapter 1 — Storyboard

**Status:** `PROPOSED`. 24 beats across 5 scenes, 42 pages.
Thumbnails go in `pages/` when drawn. Panel-level specs are generated per page on request.

**Emotional value** — the charge of the beat. This chapter deliberately runs almost flat and then
drops, once, at beat 18. A flat line for seventeen beats is the point: the reader's unease has to
come from composition and duration, not from events.

**Panel weight** — S (small) · M (medium) · L (large) · FP (full page) · SPR (spread).

## Rhythm check

- Beats 1–17 sit between 0 and +1. Beat 18 drops to −2 on a page turn and the chapter never recovers.
- Wordless pages: 20, 21, 30–33, 42 — **seven**, against `manga-style.md`'s minimum of one.
- Largest panels land on beats 11 (the lane), 23 (the hands) and 29 (the rope) — the chapter's three real subjects.
- Page-turn reveals at pp. 9, 24, 38. All land on the first panel after a turn.
- Longest stretch without dialogue: pp. 1–18 has four narration blocks and no spoken line. **The first spoken line in the series is Zakarbaal's question, on p. 36.**
