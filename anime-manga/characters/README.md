# Character Bible — CARTHAGE SAGA

**Status: `PROPOSED` roster in place.** Source story: `story/THE-STORY.md`.
Historical basis and evidence tiers: `story/historical-dossier.md`.

One file per character: `CHR-XX-<name>.md`. Copy `_TEMPLATE-character.md`.

A character's file is the only authority on how they look. If a generation disagrees with the file,
the generation is wrong — never the other way round. An established character is never redesigned
because a new prompt produced something nicer.

**Tiers in this roster:** `[A]` documented historical person · `[C]` disputed historicity ·
`[F]` invented for this series. **No invented character ever changes a historical outcome.**

## Casting rules for this series

1. **No one is designed to look like a hero.** Silhouette recognition comes from build, posture and
   costume wear, not from beauty or ornament.
2. **Ethnic and regional accuracy is continuity.** Punic, Libyan, Numidian, Iberian, Gallic, Greek
   and Italian characters are visually distinct in build, dress, hair and jewellery, and those
   distinctions are researched, not invented. See `visual/style-guide.md`.
3. **Aging is tracked in the file, not improvised.** The series spans 679 years; Hannibal ages 9→64 on
   screen.
4. **Purple is restricted** to the Carthaginian Senate and to Roman commanders. No protagonist wears
   it after ch. 18.
5. **Invented characters carry no plot armour and no plot power.** They witness, they suffer, and
   they die when the history says their part of the world died.
6. **Two narrators, and they are designed against each other.** CHR-06 Sosylos (Books One–Seven)
   is stooped, squinting, apologetic about his uncertainty, and his books are lost. CHR-35 Polybius
   (Book Eight) is upright, a horseman, audits his sourcing, and survives because he lived in the
   winners' house. Every visual axis is inverted.

## Reference set required before a character appears in a panel

1. Front (neutral, full body, canonical outfit)
2. Side / profile (full body)
3. Back (full body)
4. Face sheet — 3/4 view, front, profile
5. Expression sheet — neutral, angry, afraid, pained, joyful, determined
6. Signature poses (2–4)
7. Outfit variants (one sheet each) — **and age variants for CHR-01, 02, 04, 06**
8. Weapons / accessories (isolated, front + in-hand)
9. Colour key (even for B&W manga — it drives screentone and anime later)
