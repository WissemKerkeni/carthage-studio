## Treatments
| Treatment | Use for |
|---|---|
| `static` | Panels that read as a held image; pure composition |
| `camera` | Static art with a push, pan or rack — cheapest motion |
| `subtle` | Breathing, hair, cloth, blink; character otherwise held |
| `full` | Full character animation |
| `transition` | Match cut, wipe, whip pan between panels |
| `merged` | Several panels collapsed into one continuous shot |

## Animation budget policy
Full animation is spent on: fights, emotional turns, transformations, major reveals,
and deliberate camera moves. Everything else gets `static`, `camera` or `subtle`.
