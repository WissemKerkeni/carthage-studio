# Visual Style Bible — CARTHAGE SAGA

**Status:** `PROPOSED` in full. Nothing here is `CANON` until the author approves it.
Derived from `story/story-bible.md` §3 (seinen historical, grounded, unromantic) and §9
(no magic, no destiny, violence has aftermath).

The goal is one recognisable visual identity, not a different anime every page.
**No living artist is imitated.** Tonal neighbours are named in the story bible as register, not as
style targets, and are never named in a generation prompt.

---

## 1. Identity statement

`PROPOSED` — **A dry, sunlit, documentary-feeling historical manga: heavy blacks, a thin nervous
line, large amounts of empty sky and water, and faces drawn with restraint so that the rare moment
a face breaks costs something.** The camera behaves like a witness who is not permitted to
intervene. Ornament is reserved for the rich; the page is otherwise austere.

The three words the art department checks a page against: **weight, heat, restraint.**

## 2. Line

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Line quality | **Brush-inked, with natural taper and varying thickness within a single stroke.** Confident and economical. Ruled for architecture and ships, freehand brush for flesh, rope, cloth and rock | `CANON` — STY-01/02 |
| Line weight range | Three broad registers — heavy contour, medium interior, fine texture — achieved by brush pressure rather than by three fixed pen nibs | `CANON` — STY-01/02 |
| Contour vs interior ratio | Roughly 2:1. Figures hold their edge against busy backgrounds | `PROPOSED` |
| Hatching used for | **Texture only, and sparingly** — rope fibre, sailcloth weave, beard, cloth folds. Hatching does **not** carry the shading; screentone does (§6). Never for emotional emphasis | `CANON` — STY-01/02 |
| Background line weight | One step lighter than foreground, always. Depth is carried by line weight before it is carried by tone | `PROPOSED` |

> **`RETCON`, 2026-09-20.** This section previously specified *"three weights only: contour 1.2pt,
> interior 0.6pt, texture 0.3pt, no tapering flourish"* with hatching used for shadow. That is a
> technical-pen, European-graphic-novel specification, and it is what the first Phase 0 generations
> produced. The author chose a **Japanese seinen manga idiom** instead. Replaced above; the old
> values are recorded here and are no longer in force.

## 3. Anatomy & proportion

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Adult head-to-body | 7.5 heads. Realistic, unheroic | `PROPOSED` |
| Child ratio | 5.5–6.5 heads by age | `PROPOSED` |
| Realism level | High. Visible ribs, bad teeth, sun damage, working hands, untreated injuries | `PROPOSED` |
| Hands | **A signature of this series.** Drawn at full detail in every panel where they are visible. Rope callus, rein callus, stylus callus and oar callus are character traits, tracked in character files | `PROPOSED` |
| Chibi / super-deform | **Never.** No exceptions, including comedy beats | `PROPOSED` |
| Body diversity | Mandatory. Rowers, farmers, priests, riders and senators have different bodies, and the reader can tell a man's trade from his silhouette | `PROPOSED` |

## 4. Faces

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Eye design | Small to medium, realistic almond. **Single small high highlight.** Lower lid line always drawn. No sparkle, no star fields, no multi-highlight | `PROPOSED` |
| Eye size | Adult eye height about 1/12 of head height. Children slightly larger | `PROPOSED` |
| Nose | Fully drawn, with bridge and nostril, in every shot closer than a medium. Regional nose shapes are continuity | `PROPOSED` |
| Mouth | Drawn with a lip line. Teeth shown only when shouting, eating, or dead | `PROPOSED` |
| Expression ceiling | **Low.** Grief is a face that does not move. Maximum permitted distortion is a full open-mouthed shout, used a handful of times per Book. Sweat drops, cross-pops, nosebleeds and other gag symbols are forbidden | `PROPOSED` |
| Aging | Tracked per character file. Sun, salt and weight loss are the primary aging tools, not wrinkles alone | `PROPOSED` |

## 5. Hair

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Clumping | Medium clumps with visible individual strands at the edges. Neither glossy anime blocks nor full rendered realism | `PROPOSED` |
| Highlight | **No banded gloss highlight.** Sheen is shown by leaving paper white where the sun strikes, with no outline | `PROPOSED` |
| Motion | Heavy. Hair responds to wind late and settles fast. Nobody's hair floats | `PROPOSED` |
| Regional coding | Punic: oiled, curled, formally arranged for the rich, covered for married women. Libyan and Numidian: braided, ochre-dressed. Greek: cut short, tied. Gallic: limed and stiffened. Iberian: long, banded. **This is continuity, not decoration** | `PROPOSED` |

## 6. Shading

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Method | **Screentone carries almost all the shading**, with visible halftone dot texture — never smooth grey gradients. Solid black for weight; hatching only for texture, sparingly | `CANON` — STY-01/02 |
| Black fills (*beta*) | **Heavy and generous.** Hair is solid black; so are dark cloth, doorways, ship holds, deep shadow and night. **Heat is drawn as blown-out white; cold and fear are drawn as black** | `CANON` — STY-01/02 |
| Tone density | Three tones only — 10%, 30%, 60%. A fourth is a decision, not a habit | `PROPOSED` |
| Default key light | Hard, high, from the left unless the location file says otherwise. Mediterranean noon is the series default and it is unkind | `PROPOSED` |
| Skin | Held at paper white in sunlight, with hatched shadow only. Tone on skin means indoors, night, or illness | `PROPOSED` |

## 7. Backgrounds

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Establishing shots | Full architectural detail, ruled perspective, researched. These are the expensive panels and they earn the austerity elsewhere | `PROPOSED` |
| Dialogue panels | Sparse — a wall, a horizon, a rope, a shadow. **Empty background is permitted and encouraged**; abstract screentone fields are not | `PROPOSED` |
| Perspective rigour | Strict. Horizon and vanishing points ruled on every exterior. Architecture is never faked | `PROPOSED` |
| Emotional / abstract backgrounds | **Forbidden.** No flower fields, no speed-line voids behind a speech, no symbolic explosions. If a moment needs weight it gets a bigger panel, or an empty one | `PROPOSED` |
| Crowds | Individuals in the first two rows, silhouette beyond. Crowd density is a plot fact — a full harbour against an empty one — and is tracked | `PROPOSED` |

## 8. Effects & action language

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Speed lines | Rare and short. Used for thrown objects and falling bodies, not for running or for emotion | `PROPOSED` |
| Impact frames | One per battle, maximum. A white-on-black inversion held for a single panel | `PROPOSED` |
| Power / magic signature | **None exists.** There is no power system — `story-bible.md` §9.4 | `PROPOSED` |
| Dust, smoke, debris | Heavy and constant. Dust is this series' weather: the Volturnus wind at Cannae, the chora in August, the hemp dust of the rope walk in ch. 1 | `PROPOSED` |
| Blood | Solid black, never grey, never splashed decoratively. Wounds persist across chapters | `PROPOSED` |
| Omens | Ordinary objects — a liver, birds, a fire — framed flatly. **The camera never endorses one** (§9.4) | `PROPOSED` |

## 9. Page & panel language

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Reading direction | **Right to left**, Japanese convention | `PROPOSED` |
| Trim / aspect | B5 tankobon proportion, 1.41:1 | `PROPOSED` |
| Gutter | 6mm vertical, 4mm horizontal — wider verticals keep right-to-left order unambiguous | `PROPOSED` |
| Panels per page | 4–6 typical; 1–3 for weight; 7 or more only for rapid procedural work such as a deck or a counting house | `PROPOSED` |
| Bleed | Reserved for the sea, the Alps, Cannae, and the two pyres. Roughly one bleed per eight chapters | `PROPOSED` |
| Border variation | Solid border = present tense. **Borderless panel on white = memory.** Ragged deckle border = a story being retold by someone who was not there, used in ch. 21 and in every later "founding story retold" scene. Black gutter = death, or the sea at night | `PROPOSED` |

## 10. Lettering

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Balloon shapes | Oval = speech. Hard-edged rectangle = Sosylos' narration. Borderless text on art = thought. Spiked = shouting. **No wobbly-outline "weak voice" balloons** | `PROPOSED` |
| Tails | Short, straight, single. No curling | `PROPOSED` |
| Dialogue font | Upright sans, medium weight. Narration in a lighter, slightly wider face so it reads as a different voice | `PROPOSED` |
| SFX | Hand drawn, integrated into the art, **low volume**. This series has very few sound effects and they land hard when they come. Katakana in the Japanese edition, hand-lettered Latin in translation | `PROPOSED` |
| Language coding | Punic is unmarked and is the default. Greek in angle brackets. Latin in double angle brackets. Libyan and Numidian in corner brackets. Gaulish and Iberian left untranslated, glyphs only, whenever the point-of-view character does not understand them — `story-bible.md` §9.9 | `PROPOSED` |

## 11. Colour

For covers, key art, and the anime phase.

| Decision | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | Sun-bleached: bone white, dust ochre, terracotta, olive drab, rope tan, and the sea in two blues — near-turquoise shallow, near-black deep | `PROPOSED` |
| Saturation | Low, except for three things which are always fully saturated: **Tyrian purple, blood, and fire** | `PROPOSED` |
| Signature accent | **Tyrian purple**, restricted by casting rule 4 to the Senate and to Roman commanders. Its fading across Book One is Elissa's visual arc — see `CHR-03` | `PROPOSED` |
| Rome's palette | Iron grey, red, undyed wool. Deliberately duller and more uniform than Carthage's, which is varied, foreign and expensive-looking | `PROPOSED` |

## 12. Negative constraints

Things this series never does visually.

1. No chibi, no gag symbols, no sweat drops, no cross-popping veins.
2. No abstract emotional backgrounds; no flower or sparkle fields.
3. No glamour lighting, no wind machines, no beauty shots.
4. No fan-service framing. Nudity appears only where the scene is about bodies being used — slavery, wounds, the dead, the baths — and is drawn flatly.
5. No costume or armour that is not researched. No generic "ancient warrior" kit.
6. No supernatural confirmation of any kind, ever.
7. No anachronism: no stirrups, no horseshoes, no oversized elephants (§9.10), no crossbows, no mail before the Gauls.
8. No panel that makes killing look weightless.
