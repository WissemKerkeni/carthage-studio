# Timeline — CARTHAGE SAGA

**Status:** `PROPOSED`. Two clocks: the world's history, and the story's own elapsed time.
Continuity checks (ages, scars, seasons, who-knows-what) resolve against this file.
**4 seasons · 8 Books · 200 chapters · c. 825–146 BC.**
Evidence tiers: `[A]` documented · `[B]` likely · `[C]` disputed · `[F]` invented.

## Era scale

The series uses **real BC dates** internally. On screen, **no year is ever stated** — characters
date things by suffetes, harvests, wars and the ages of their children, which is how they actually
did it. Production documents use BC; scripts use in-world dating.

In-world dating conventions the scripts may use:
- **"the year of the suffetes X and Y"** `[A]` — the standard Punic civil dating.
- **"the summer after the great hail"** — Libyan and rural reckoning `[B]`.
- Romans: **"in the consulship of..."** `[A]`.
- Greeks: Olympiads `[A]`.

**Scipio Aemilianus' descent — continuity-critical, and all documented `[A]`:**
natural son of **L. Aemilius Paullus Macedonicus**; natural grandson of the **L. Aemilius Paullus
killed at Cannae** (ch. 156); adoptive grandson of **Scipio Africanus** (ch. 146–183). The man who
burns Carthage is descended from the consul Hannibal destroyed *and* from the man who beat him.
Do not let a script treat this as invented.
